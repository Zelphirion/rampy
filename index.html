Graphics & Performance
Everything here applies the instant you move it, and the world is rendering behind this panel — so watch it (and the frame rate) as you go. The settings are remembered on this device.
Preset Default
1.00×
1.00× the display's own pixel ratio.
Lower is blockier and faster. Below 0.5 is a deliberately chunky, retro look — the bottom of the slider is there for old machines.
500 units
The world is always drawn to the horizon; this only moves if you change it here.
Shadows
Shadow maps cast by the world's suns. Turning them off is the biggest frame-rate win on this screen.
Anti-aliasing
Smooths jagged edges at a fill-rate cost. It is baked into the canvas, so switching it rebuilds the renderer — expect a one-frame hitch.
Frame rate
Frames per second right now, averaged over the last half second. Drive somewhere busy and watch it dip.
—fps
Automatic quality On
If the frame rate stays under 25fps, the settings step themselves down until it recovers, saving as it goes. Choosing Max above turns this off and keeps the picture you asked for.
